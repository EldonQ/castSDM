# AI-assisted maintenance record

On 7 November 2026, OpenAI Codex assisted changes for castSDM 0.27.0/0.27.1/0.28.0, including
diagnostic interfaces, score and validation semantics, scenario reductions,
raster I/O, source-preserving cache fingerprints, examples, tests, benchmarks
and documentation. Changed R sources
carry a short annotation referring to this record. Exact application build and
model identifiers were not exposed in this maintenance session and are not
invented here. Authors should record identifiers available in their own client
when preparing a journal disclosure.

Evidence consists of executable tests, package checks and bounded benchmark
artifacts; see the corresponding maintenance report for their actual outcomes.
AI-generated reasoning is not independent scientific evidence. No new field
observations, external validation labels, model superiority or editorial
acceptance are asserted. The completed real-case study was not rerun during
maintenance. The author remains responsible for reviewing code, assumptions,
data permissions, scientific conclusions and the final disclosure wording.

This record covers the named maintenance changes; it is not a claim that no
other part of the project previously used AI assistance.

The 0.28.0 restoration reuses the established cartography and original numbered
figure layouts, adds a complete figure/export interface and caches diagnostic
products separately from rendering. Existing scientific fits and predictions
are preserved; figure tests include native-grid classification and proof that
render-only output does not call a fitted engine.

installed-package scenario parity, startup isolation and preserved scientific
cache identities; no full real-case research pipeline is rerun for this fix.
